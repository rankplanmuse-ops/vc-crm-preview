Import CSV

Bring a firm and contact list in as a spreadsheet. Rows are grouped by firm domain, one review card is created per firm, and filing runs the same merge rules as a crawl: existing records are never overwritten and every email needs its source.

Columns it understands

Firm: Name, Website or Domain (one is required), HQ, Region, Sectors or Niche, AUM, Founded, AI program, AI evidence.

People: Contact name (or Named contact), Role, Email / Emails (all) (semicolons separate several), Email source / Source URL / Contact page, Contact route, LinkedIn. The Lightning CRM exports import as-is.

The rule from the crawl applies here too: an email with no source URL on its row is not imported as an email. It is counted, noted on the review card, and can be filed once you add the source there.
